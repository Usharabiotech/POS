Store setup onboarding — what we need from you

This form collects everything we need to set up your billing system and get you serving customers. Fill in what you can, attach the files we ask for, and send it back — that is what starts your setup.

How to fill it

  • Type your answers in the boxes, or print this and write them in — whatever is easier.
  • Items tagged Required are needed to go live. Everything else we can switch on later.
  • Send any files we ask for (logo, menu, photos) with your reply.
  • If something does not apply, just write N/A — there are no wrong answers.

What happens after you send it back

  1. You return this form with your details and files.
  2. We build your store — menu, prices, logins, receipt design (about 2–4 working days).
  3. We run a 30-minute training call and hand it over.
  4. You go live — and we stay on support.

10

WhatsApp bills & customer loyalty

Two optional features customers love. WhatsApp bill sending needs a WhatsApp Business number with API access (we help set this up). Loyalty needs no extra setup — say yes and the till starts recording repeat customers and points.

11

Reports & end-of-day

At day close, the app downloads the day's sales, bills, and a summary to the billing device — your own permanent copy. Totals stay in the app; detailed bills are kept lean in the cloud to keep running costs low.
